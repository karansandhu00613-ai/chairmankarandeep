Common Questions

Do I need external APIs?

No. Karan is completely self-contained. It doesn't rely on ChatGPT, Claude, or any external AI. All services run on your infrastructure.

Can I run this locally?

Yes! Just run docker-compose up and access at http://localhost:8000. Perfect for development and testing.

How much does it cost?

Self-hosted is free forever. Cloud hosting is $29/month (Pro plan). No hidden fees. No per-user charges.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Data is stored on your infrastructure or private GitHub repo. No third-party access. Full encryption support. PBKDF2 password hashing.

Can I customize it?

Absolutely. Full source code included. Modify anything. Add features. Deploy on your servers. Complete control.

How do I integrate with Slack/Discord?

Add webhook URLs to environment variables. Karan automatically sends alerts and notifications to your channels.
